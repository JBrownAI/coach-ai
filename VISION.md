# CoachAI — Vision

## Mission

Build an AI-assisted personal training platform that combines modern artificial intelligence with the practical knowledge gained from 15+ years and approximately 15,000–20,000 personal training sessions.

## Problem

Generic AI systems can generate exercise programs, but they don't necessarily reason like an experienced personal trainer.

CoachAI aims to capture the decision-making process behind effective coaching.

## Initial Goals

- Create individualized training recommendations
- Adapt programs based on client feedback
- Explain the reasoning behind recommendations
- Track client progress
- Help trainers save time
- Eventually learn from real-world coaching decisions

## Founder Background

The project is being developed by a personal trainer with:

- 7+ years of professional personal training experience
- Approximately 15,000–20,000 training sessions
- Bachelor's degree in Integrative Physiology
- Air Force technical maintenance experience
- Experience building and troubleshooting computers
- Experience with complex technical systems

## Long-Term Vision

Create an AI coaching system that assists—not blindly replaces—experienced human coaches.

The long-term objective is to develop a system capable of reasoning about training decisions rather than simply generating exercise lists.
